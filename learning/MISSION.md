# Mission: Reinforcement Learning through a chess engine

## Why
The owner already works with machine learning and RL is the next area to add. The
goal is to understand the field well enough to recognise when RL fits a business
problem, choose an approach, anticipate its failure modes, and direct an AI/agentic
implementation of it. Hand-implementing algorithms is not the goal; understanding is.

## Success looks like
- Can explain the main RL families (value-based, policy-gradient, actor-critic,
  model-based, search-augmented) and when each is the right tool.
- Can read a training run (loss, entropy, value error, win/draw rates) and diagnose
  what is going wrong and what to change.
- Knows the edge cases and traps: sparse rewards, non-stationarity in self-play,
  credit assignment, reward hacking, exploration collapse, sample efficiency.
- Can map a business problem to an RL formulation (state, action, reward, horizon)
  or argue why RL is the wrong choice.
- Can direct an AI agent to implement an RL method and judge whether the result is
  correct by its behaviour, not by reading every line.

## Constraints
- No time constraints; work proceeds at whatever pace the sessions allow.
- Hardware: CPU-only Apple Silicon Mac with unified memory. Experiments must fit
  this; no GPU assumptions.
- Learning preference: practical over theoretical. Theory only as needed to make a
  decision or read a result. Decisions backed by cited sources, not recall.

## Out of scope
- Nothing explicitly excluded. Topics are prioritised by the roadmap and by what the
  current experiments make relevant.

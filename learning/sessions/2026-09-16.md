## Lessons (records 0006-0011)
6. Start-state curricula need failed attempts to end quickly; otherwise attempts,
   not updates, are the bottleneck. Split metrics by episode source.
7. A non-zero start potential shifts all returns by -scale*Phi; the advantage and
   the policy are untouched (Corollary 2 again).
8. A colour asymmetry was exposure (walking into mates), not finishing; sample
   size was tested and rejected with a second seed.

9. Orienting the board makes mirror positions one picture; move ids in OpenSpiel
   are already relative, so only observations change.

10. Ask of every held-out set: whose positions? Of every game count: how did
    the games end? Attempts, not set size, drive accuracy.
11. On-distribution puzzles raised on-distribution accuracy only; games collapse
    into rule draws as the policy sharpens (entropy 0.24).

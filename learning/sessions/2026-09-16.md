## Lessons (records 0006-0011)
6. Start-state curricula need failed attempts to end quickly; otherwise attempts,
   not updates, are the bottleneck. Split metrics by episode source.
7. A non-zero start potential shifts all returns by -scale*Phi; the advantage and
   the policy are untouched (Corollary 2 again).
8. A colour asymmetry was exposure (walking into mates), not finishing; sample
   size was tested and rejected with a second seed.

9. Orienting the board makes mirror positions one picture; move ids in OpenSpiel
   are already relative, so only observations change.

10. Ask of every held-out set: whose positions? Of every game count: how did
    the games end? Attempts, not set size, drive accuracy.
11. On-distribution puzzles raised on-distribution accuracy only; games collapse
    into rule draws as the policy sharpens (entropy 0.24).

## PPO with GAE (evening)

Decided in chat: both switches, three arms, 64-ply rollouts for all, fresh
network, paper hyperparameters. Owner parked the comprehension questions and the
prediction ("for now skip questions and implement"). Implemented
`add-ppo-gae`: rollout re-evaluation, `compute_gae_for_model`, `update_model`,
presets, diagnostics. Two bugs surfaced: train-mode BatchNorm broke the ratio
(clip fraction 0.52 before any learning) and forced moves reported normalised
entropy 12. One reading: at lr 3e-4 PPO leaves the clip band after 5 of 16
minibatches, so a fourth arm ran at 1e-4. Results in
`experiments/ppo-gae/` and the proposal's Outcome section: PPO 0.44 / 0.42
Lichess vs A2C 0.32 at equal plies, lr 1e-4 kept opening entropy above 0.5,
no arm changed the game collapse (200-ply draws, 5-9% in-game mate). Owner set
the standard budget to 7,500 plies per board.

12. A2C is bounded by gradient steps, not by moves played; bigger rollouts with
    one step waste data. PPO's reuse is the fix, and clipping is what makes the
    reuse safe.
13. An importance ratio must depend only on parameters; batch statistics in the
    forward pass break it.

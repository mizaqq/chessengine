## Lessons (records 0001-0005)
1. The policy learns from the advantage, not the return.
2. Material only matters while the game continues; terminal potential is 0.
3. Under shaping the value head reads V - material; add material back to judge it.
4. Smaller shaping scale flipped the value/material correlation negative (predicted).
5. Models know a few mating pictures (top-1, p>0.5) but miss 96% of mates-in-one;
   greedy play loops into repetition draws.
